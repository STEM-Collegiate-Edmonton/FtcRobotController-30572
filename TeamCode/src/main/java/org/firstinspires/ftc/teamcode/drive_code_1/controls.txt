Team 30572 Teleop V1 controls

== Single driver ==

Left Stick X  - Strafe (Lateral)
Left Stick Y  - Forward (Axial)
Right Stick X - Rotate (Heading)

Left Trigger  - Intake reverse
Right Trigger - Intake forward

Hold B - Autolaunch

Right Bumper + X - Toggle field relative drive
Left Bumper + X  - Reset IMU (for field relative)

Dpad Up/Down - Manual windmill control


== Two drivers ==

Driver 1 - Drive, field relative control, and intake
Driver 2 - Launch and manual windmill control
